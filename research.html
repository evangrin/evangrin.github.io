Network measurement · course project

Analysis of Internet Traffic Through Major US Backbone Links

Internet measurement and analysis of high-traffic backbone links across the world give us key insight into how the Internet is used in real-world scenarios. In this paper, we study Internet traffic across three links distributed across the United States, examining how usage has changed over time and varies by region. Specifically, we analyze international and domestic traffic, changes in the use of secure protocols over time, and other general measurements such as the adoption of IPv6.

Convex optimization · ECE 273

Blind Deconvolution

Blind deconvolution is the problem of recovering two signals given the output of their convolution. In this paper, we describe the problem, discuss the convex blind-deconvolution method of Ahmed et al., and implement their algorithms and experiments.

We implement the Ivy distributed shared memory system in C, based on the 1989 paper by Kai Li and Paul Hudak, “Memory Coherence in Shared Virtual Memory Systems.”
