About

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

I am broadly interested in understanding the capabilities and fundamental limitations of algorithms and learning systems. I am especially interested in applying this perspective to the theoretical foundations of AI fairness and safety, where classical questions about complexity, information, and strategic interaction arise in problems of auditing, alignment, and oversight.
